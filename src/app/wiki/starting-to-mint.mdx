# Starting to Mint 🚀

Nominated and approved? Run a synced node, join the MINTER group, then add your minting account through **Q-Node** in Qortal Hub.

> Not approved yet? Start with [Q-Mintership](/wiki/q-mintership).

⚠️ Use **Qortal Hub** and the **Q-Node** Q-App — the legacy Qortal UI is deprecated.

## Overview

1. **Sync your node** in Qortal Hub
2. **Join the MINTER group** — accept your invite
3. **Add a minting account** in Q-Node
4. **Stay online** until you reach Level 1 (~7,200 online blocks)

## Step 1 — Sync Your Node

1. Install **Qortal Hub** from [qortal.dev/downloads](https://qortal.dev/downloads) — official releases only
2. Let the node **fully sync** before minting
3. **Bootstrap** if syncing from scratch is slow — see [Running a Node](/wiki/running-a-node)

## Step 2 — Join the MINTER Group

Once admins have activated your invite, accept it in Qortal Hub with a **JOIN_GROUP** transaction. After it confirms on-chain, you are in the MINTER group.

## Step 3 — Add a Minting Account (Q-Node)

Open **Q-Node** from the Q-Apps dashboard in Hub:

<figure>
  <img src="/images/Wiki/QortalHubQ-AppsDashboard.png" alt="Qortal Hub Q-Apps dashboard" style={{ width: "100%", maxWidth: "900px" }} />
  <figcaption>Open Q-Node from the Q-Apps dashboard in Qortal Hub</figcaption>
</figure>

1. Choose **Add Minting Account** and select your approved account
2. Confirm the transaction — Q-Node creates and assigns your minting key automatically
3. Once confirmed, your `blocksMinted` count begins climbing

No manual reward-share steps needed — Q-Node handles it in one action.

## Step 4 — Reach Level 1

Stay online. Level 1 arrives after roughly **7,200 online blocks**, though credits batch every ~1,000 blocks so it may take ~8,000–9,000 blocks in practice. Wait until Hub shows **Level 1**, not just the raw counter.

## Keep Your Account Safe

- Treat your minting key carefully — it lets a node mint for your account
- Back up your account file and password offline — loss is permanent
- To move nodes, regenerate or re-add your minting key on the new machine

## Best Practices

### Node reliability

- Run 24/7 — only open Hub when managing things
- Use stable power and internet (UPS helps)
- Keep Core updated and monitor sync height
- Bootstrap if you fall badly out of sync
- Set up port forwarding or uPnP for better peer connectivity

### Security

- Download only from **qortal.org**, **qortal.dev**, or the official GitHub
- Never share your backup file, password, or minting key
- Ignore anyone offering to sell or fast-track minting rights

### One minting account per person

You may have unlimited non-minting accounts, but only **one minting account**. Multiple minting accounts means **all of them get removed** from the MINTER group.

## Nodes & Hardware

Minting is lightweight — **uptime** matters more than raw specs.

| Component | Minimum | Recommended |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CPU | Dual-core | Quad-core+ |
| RAM | 4 GB | 8 GB+ |
| Storage | SSD | Larger SSD with room to grow |
| Network | Stable broadband | Wired + port forwarding |
| Uptime | As much as possible | 24/7 |

**Options:** home PC, **QORTector** appliance, VPS, or single-board computer (use an SSD, not an SD card).

You can run **multiple nodes** with the **same minting account** for redundancy — that is encouraged. Just never run more than one minting account.
