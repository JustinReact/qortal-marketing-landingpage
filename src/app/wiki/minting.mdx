# Minting on Qortal ⛏️

Minting is how the Qortal blockchain produces blocks and stays decentralized. Unlike mining or staking, it is not about money or hardware power — it is about people running nodes and supporting the network.

## What Is Minting?

On most blockchains, new blocks are produced by **mining** (Proof of Work) or **staking** (Proof of Stake) — both ultimately give the most power to whoever has the most money.

Qortal does something different. Block production is called **minting**, performed by **minters**: accounts granted minting rights that run a full node. Every online minter contributes to each block, and rewards are shared among online minters rather than going to a single winner. You cannot buy your way to more influence — influence is earned through participation over time.

> Minting is about **supporting the network and infrastructure**, not extracting profit. Qortal is sovereignty-driven, not money-driven.

⚠️ **One minting account per person.** Running multiple minting accounts to multiply rewards is not allowed. The **Minter Admins** actively watch for this, and if the rule is broken, **all related accounts will be removed from the MINTER group**.

## Why Minting Matters

- **Decentralization & security.** More independent minters running nodes around the world means a more resilient, censorship-resistant network.
- **Fair rewards.** Block rewards are distributed across all online minters each block, instead of being captured by the largest holder or the most powerful machine.
- **On-chain influence.** Minters accumulate `blocksMinted`, which translates into a minting **level**. Levels feed into Qortal's polling/voting system, giving long-term contributors a meaningful voice in network decisions.

## Levels & Influence

Each block a minter's node helps create increases that account's `blocksMinted` count. As this grows, the account climbs through minting **levels**. Higher levels carry more weight in the network's polling system and unlock responsibilities — for example, only **Level 5+** accounts can nominate new minters.

| Concept | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| `blocksMinted` | Total blocks your account has helped mint. Permanent and cumulative — it never resets. |
| Level | Derived from `blocksMinted`. Determines voting influence and certain permissions (e.g. nominating). |
| Level 1 | Reached after roughly 7,200 online blocks. The first full minting milestone. |
| Level 5+ | Experienced minters eligible to **nominate** new candidates for mintership. |

⚠️ **Block credits are batched.** Since a December 2023 performance change, minted-block credits are applied roughly every **1,000 blocks** rather than block-by-block. The 7,200 blocks needed for Level 1 has not changed, but in practice it can take around 8,000–9,000 blocks of online time before Level 1 shows. Always wait until Qortal Hub actually displays **Level 1** — do not rely on the raw block counter alone.

## From Sponsorship to Nominations

Qortal **no longer uses sponsorship** to onboard minters. The old model — where a Level 5+ account issued you a reward-share key to mint your way to Level 1 — has been replaced.

The current system is **Mintership**, and the way you get into it is the **Nomination** process, managed through the **Q-Mintership** Q-App and the community-elected **Minter Admins**. Under Nominations, you no longer submit yourself — a **Level 5+ minter nominates you**, the Minter Admins vote, and group-approval transactions deliver the **MINTER group invite** — all verifiable on the Qortal blockchain.

See [Q-Mintership](/wiki/q-mintership) for the full nomination flow, and [Starting to Mint](/wiki/starting-to-mint) for what to do after approval.

## History of Qortal Minting

Every change to Qortal's minting model was driven by the same principle: **no one should be able to buy power over the network**.

| Era | Who decides | Key weakness it solved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| QORA (PoS) | Money / stake | — |
| Genesis (Forgers) | Prior contribution | Removed PoS; fair start |
| Sponsorship | A single Level 5+ sponsor | Onboarding without buying in |
| Mintership | Self-created cards + Minter Admins | Decentralized, transparent approval |
| **Nominations (current)** | Level 5+ nominator + Minter Admins | Adds vouching / web-of-trust |

**2014 — QORA:** Qortal's story starts with QORA, which used Proof of Stake. PoS lets money buy influence — the opposite of Qortal's egalitarian ethos.

**2016–2020 — QORA → Qortal:** The codebase was rebuilt from scratch with **no pre-mine**. QORA holders who burned their coins were credited toward a gradual QORT conversion.

**2020 — Genesis minters:** QORA Forgers and QORTector purchasers were grandfathered in as founding minters.

**2020–2024 — Sponsorship:** Level 5+ accounts issued 0% reward-share keys to newcomers. Sponsors earned nothing extra — it was purely about vouching. Replaced because it leaned on personal connections without transparent, community-wide oversight.

**2024–2025 — Mintership:** Community-elected **Minter Admins** took over approvals using on-chain `GROUP_APPROVAL` transactions. Candidates published cards on the Minter Board; ~40% admin approval triggered a MINTER group invite.

**Current — Nominations:** Level 5+ minters nominate candidates instead of candidates submitting themselves. This adds a **web-of-trust** layer — an established minter stakes their reputation by vouching for a real person they know.

## Minting Rewards and Tiers

The more you mint, the more your level increases — up to level 10. Higher levels receive greater shares of the block rewards. Minting is how blocks are found and the chain progresses. Without minters, the chain does not move.

- **Levels 1 and 2** share 6% of the block reward
- **Levels 3 and 4** share 13% of the block reward
- **Levels 5 and 6** share 19% of the block reward
- **Levels 7 and 8** share 26% of the block reward
- **Levels 9 and 10** share 32% of the block reward (currently inactive — no minters at this tier yet)
- QORA holders transitioning into QORT share 1% of the block reward
- Minter and Dev Admins share a minimum of 3% of the block reward, plus the Tier 5 share while that tier has no active minters (see [Q-Mintership](/wiki/q-mintership#minter-admins) for details)

There are no more Founder rewards. Founder accounts are no different than any other minting account.

## Block Rewards

Block rewards on Qortal are meant to be never-ending. There is no total cap on QORT in circulation. Approximately every **6 months**, the block reward decreases by 0.25 QORT from its start at 5 QORT, until 2 QORT is the reward. Thereafter, a vote can adjust the reward by 0.25 to 1 QORT difference per block, once per year. Block reward can only be voted as low as 1 QORT or as high as 5 QORT per block.

## Summary

Minting is at the heart of Qortal's ecosystem. Whether you are running a Raspberry Pi or a full desktop, your consistent presence strengthens the network. To become a minter today, build a real track record on Qortal, get nominated by a Level 5+ minter, and earn approval from the Minter Admins — then follow the [Starting to Mint](/wiki/starting-to-mint) guide to get your node minting.
