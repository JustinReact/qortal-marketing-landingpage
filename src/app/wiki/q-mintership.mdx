# Q-Mintership 🗳️

Potential minters are **nominated by Level 5+ community members** and **approved by Minter Admins** through on-chain voting. This replaced the old sponsorship model and the earlier approach where candidates submitted their own cards.

Open the **Q-Mintership** Q-App in Qortal Hub to follow along.

## The Nomination Flow

1. **Nomination** — A Level 5+ minter publishes a nomination card on the Minter Board.
2. **Voting** — A poll is attached. Community and Minter Admin votes are tracked separately.
3. **~40% admin yes votes** — Action buttons unlock on the card for admins.
4. **Group invite** — An admin creates a MINTER group invite; ~40% of admins must sign it via group approval.
5. **Join and mint** — The candidate accepts the invite, adds a minting account in Q-Node, and starts minting.

## Becoming a Minter

You cannot buy minting rights or approve yourself. A **Level 5+ minter must nominate you**, and **Minter Admins** must approve you on-chain.

### What You Need

- **Account** with a registered Qortal name
- **Full node** via Qortal Hub, synced and online
- **Track record** of real network usage over time
- **A nominator** — a Level 5+ minter willing to vouch for you

Register your name in Qortal Hub first. Names let you publish to QDN and give admins a consistent identity to evaluate.

### How to Get Nominated

1. **Use the network visibly** — publish to QDN, participate in Q-Chat, send Q-Mail, use Q-Apps. On-chain activity is your evidence.
2. **Engage with the community** — nominations work best when someone who knows you vouches for you.
3. **Contribute** — build Q-Apps, help newcomers, create content, report issues.
4. **Learn the system** — understand minting, levels, and Qortal's values.
5. **Find a Level 5+ nominator** — you do not apply yourself; someone vouches _for_ you.

## The Nomination Process

Everything happens inside **Q-Mintership** and is recorded on the Qortal blockchain.

### Two Approval Stages

| Stage | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Poll (~40% admin yes)** | Unlocks admin action buttons on the nomination card |
| **Group approval (~40% signatures)** | Activates the MINTER group invite on-chain |

With roughly **21 Minter Admins** today, ~40% means about **9 signatures**. This number changes as admins are added or removed.

### Step by Step

1. A Level 5+ minter publishes a **nomination card** on the Minter Board.
2. A **poll** opens — admin votes drive the on-chain process.
3. Admins **review, discuss, and vote** on the candidate's track record.
4. At ~40% admin yes votes, **action buttons appear** on the card.
5. An admin creates a **pending group invite** to the MINTER group.
6. Admins sign the invite; at ~40% signatures it becomes **active**.
7. The candidate issues **JOIN_GROUP** in Qortal Hub.
8. The new minter adds a minting account in **Q-Node** and begins minting. See [Starting to Mint](/wiki/starting-to-mint).

The poll stage creates a public record of sentiment. The group-approval stage is the cryptographic authorization — no single admin can invite someone alone.

Removals work the same way: a quorum of Minter Admins must approve via group approval.

## Publishing a Nomination

For **Level 5+ minters** nominating someone else. When you nominate, you are vouching for them — your reputation is on the line.

### Before You Nominate

- Confirm you are Level 5+
- Confirm your candidate has a **registered name**
- Gather their address and links to QDN publications or contributions
- Have a clear, honest reason they should mint
- Give the candidate a heads-up so they can accept the invite when approved

### In Q-Mintership

1. Open **Q-Mintership** in Qortal Hub
2. Go to the **Minter Board**
3. **Create a nomination card** with the candidate's details
4. **Write the nomination** — who they are, how you know them, what they've done, links to evidence
5. **Publish** and confirm the transaction
6. **Support the nomination** — answer admin questions on the card

### What to Include

- **Who** — registered name and a one-line intro
- **Relationship** — how you know them or their work
- **Contributions** — apps, content, community help, time active
- **Evidence** — links to QDN resources, Q-Apps, videos
- **Why mint** — why they want to support the network

Do not nominate strangers or inflate claims. Admins verify what they can, and a weak nomination reflects on you too.

## Writing a Strong Nomination

**Do:** cite real relationships, link to verifiable on-chain activity, explain why they value Qortal, stay available for admin questions.

**Don't:** write vague one-liners, focus on earning QORT, nominate as a favor, disappear after publishing.

Because Qortal has no KYC, **on-chain activity** is how admins confirm you are a real person:

1. **Publishing to QDN** (strongest) — Q-Share, Q-Tube, Q-Apps, websites via QWB. Publishes stay on-chain permanently.
2. **Groups and Chat** — good engagement, but chat is not kept long-term on-chain.
3. **Viewing content** — useful for you, but not visible as evidence to admins.

Include links to your nominee's published work on the nomination card. **Quality beats quantity.**

## Minter Admins

Community-elected members who approve and remove minting rights via on-chain group approval. Being an admin is a paid responsibility — it does not grant extra voting influence beyond what you earn by minting.

### What They Do

- Vote on nomination cards (separate from community votes)
- Sign group invites and removals
- Deliberate privately on the encrypted Admin Board

### Boards in Q-Mintership

| Board | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Minter Board** | Public — nominations, discussion, voting |
| **Admin Board** | Private — admin decisions and removals |
| **MAM / AR Board** | Adding or removing Minter Admins |

### Rewards

Minter Admins receive a share of block rewards (~17.5% currently, split evenly among admins). **Admins must be actively minting** to receive their share. Dev Admins govern Core updates separately and have no minting requirement.

### Becoming a Minter Admin

Level 5+ only. There is no application form — you earn trust by contributing consistently, then get proposed and approved on the MAM/AR board through group approval.

## Key Terms

- **Nomination** — A Level 5+ minter vouching for a candidate via a card on the Minter Board
- **MINTER group** — On-chain group whose members can mint
- **Group approval** — Quorum-based signatures (~40% of Minter Admins) required for invites and removals
- **Level 5+** — Experienced minters who can nominate new candidates
- **Q-Node** — Q-App in Hub for adding and managing minting accounts
